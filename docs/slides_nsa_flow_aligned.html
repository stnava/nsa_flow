1. Formulation & Non-Negative Stiefel Geometry

Why non-negativity and orthogonality require a continuous relaxation

Foundation

The NSA-Flow Energy Functional

$$\min_{Y \ge 0} \; E_w(Y) = (1 - w) \underbrace{\frac{\|Y - X_0\|_F^2}{\|X_0\|_F^2}}_{\text{Fidelity } F(Y)} \;+\; w \underbrace{\frac{\|G - \frac{1}{k} I_k\|_F^2}{1 - \frac{1}{k}}}_{\text{Defect } \tilde{D}(Y)}$$

Where $G = \frac{Y^\top Y}{\mathrm{tr}(Y^\top Y)}$ is the trace-normalized Gram matrix. Both terms are dimensionless and $\mathcal{O}(1)$ by construction, making $w \in [0, 1]$ a true calibration-free convex weight.

Proposition: The Rigid Geometry of Non-Negative Orthogonality

  • Zero Set: $\tilde{D}(Y) = 0 \iff Y^\top Y = c I_k$. The columns are pairwise orthogonal and have equal norms.
  • Disjoint Support: Because $Y \ge 0$, $\langle y_i, y_j \rangle = 0 \iff y_{ri} y_{rj} = 0 \; \forall r$. Every feature belongs to at most one component!
  • The Disjoint Disconnect: The fully constrained set $\mathbb{R}_{>0} \cdot \mathrm{St}(p,k) \cap \{Y \ge 0\}$ is a stratified set of dimension $p-k$. Its top strata are isolated combinatorial clusterings, joined only through lower-dimensional rank-deficient boundaries.

The Need for Relaxation

A descent algorithm confined strictly to the Stiefel manifold cannot jump between clusterings. NSA-Flow relaxes the manifold constraint into a smooth penalty $\tilde{D}(Y)$, allowing continuous descent from data reconstruction ($w=0$) to structured disjoint clustering ($w \to 1$).

2. The Symmetry Mismatch: Why Anchored Fidelity Over-Constrains

The conflict between right-$O(k)$ invariance of defect and rigid coordinate anchoring

The Problem
✓ Defect $\tilde{D}(Y)$ is Right-$O(k)$ Invariant
$$D(Y Q) = D(Y) \quad \forall Q \in O(k)$$

Gram matrix transforms by conjugation: $G(YQ) = Q^\top G(Y) Q$. The Frobenius norm $\|Q^\top G Q - \frac{1}{k} I\|_F^2$ is invariant. Orthogonality cares only about internal angles, not orientation!

✕ Anchored Fidelity $F(Y)$ is Rigid
$$F(Y Q) \ne F(Y) \quad (Q \ne I_k)$$

$\|Y - X_0\|_F^2$ charges full price for any rotation within the subspace. It forces $Y$ to retain the exact arbitrary coordinate axes of $X_0$.

The Statistical Trap with Anchored Fidelity (`align=False`)

When $X_0$ is obtained from PCA or SVD, only its column span $\mathrm{span}(X_0)$ is statistically meaningful. The individual axes are arbitrary artifacts of eigenvalue ordering and arbitrary coordinate basis convention.

  • To achieve non-negativity and disjoint support ($D \to 0$), $Y$ must rotate.
  • Under align=False, the fidelity term resists this rotation with force proportional to $(1-w)$.
  • Result: $Y$ gets trapped. You pay a heavy accuracy penalty ($F$) simply to preserve an arbitrary rotation that the orthogonality term doesn't even care about!

The Question

Can we make the fidelity term invariant to rotations in $O(k)$, measuring distance to the span of $X_0$ rather than the point $X_0$?

align=False punishes rotation; align=True permits rotation within span

3. Procrustes Alignment (`aligned=True`): Quotienting Out Rotation

Replacing distance-to-point with distance-to-orbit via Orthogonal Procrustes

The Solution

Aligned Fidelity: Distance to the Orbit

$$F_{\text{aligned}}(Y) = \min_{Q \in O(k)} \frac{\|Y - X_0 Q\|_F^2}{\|X_0\|_F^2}$$

Since $\|X_0 Q\|_F = \|X_0\|_F$ for any $Q \in O(k)$, the denominator is unaltered. The term remains strictly dimensionless, exactly zero whenever $Y$ is any orthogonal rotation of $X_0$.

Closed-Form Solution via $k \times k$ SVD

Expanding the Frobenius norm:

$$\|Y - X_0 Q\|_F^2 = \|Y\|_F^2 + \|X_0\|_F^2 - 2 \, \mathrm{tr}\bigl(Q^\top (X_0^\top Y)\bigr)$$

Let $M = X_0^\top Y \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times k}$ with singular value decomposition $M = U \Sigma V^\top$:

  • Optimal Rotation: $Q^* = U V^\top \in O(k)$ (polar factor of $X_0^\top Y$).
  • Aligned Target: $X_0^{\text{align}} = X_0 Q^* = X_0 U V^\top$.
  • Nuclear Norm Identity: $\max_{Q \in O(k)} \mathrm{tr}(Q^\top M) = \mathrm{tr}(\Sigma) = \|X_0^\top Y\|_*$.
$$F_{\text{aligned}}(Y) = \frac{\|Y\|_F^2 + \|X_0\|_F^2 - 2 \|X_0^\top Y\|_*}{\|X_0\|_F^2}$$

Negligible Computational Cost

Computing $Q^*$ requires only one $k \times k$ SVD per step. For $k=5$ or $10$ and $p=1000$, $k \times k$ SVD takes $\approx 3\,\mu\mathrm{s}$, completely negligible next to the $\mathcal{O}(p k^2)$ matrix multiplications of the solver.

4. The Gradient via Envelope Theorem (Zero SVD Differentiation)

Why aligning introduces zero numerical instability or autograd bottlenecks

Numerics & Gradient

The Classical SVD Gradient Trap

Normally, differentiating through torch.linalg.svd divides by singular value differences: $$\frac{\partial U}{\partial M} \propto \frac{1}{\sigma_i^2 - \sigma_j^2}$$ Whenever singular values coalesce ($\sigma_i \approx \sigma_j$), the backward pass explodes or returns NaNs. This is common when matrices become orthogonal.

Danskin's / Envelope Theorem: SVD Derivative Vanishes!

Since $Q^*(Y)$ is the interior global minimizer of $Q \mapsto \|Y - X_0 Q\|_F^2$:

$$\left. \nabla_Q \|Y - X_0 Q\|_F^2 \right|_{Q = Q^*} = 0$$

By the chain rule, the total derivative with respect to $Y$ is:

$$\frac{d}{dY} F_{\text{aligned}}(Y) = \frac{\partial F}{\partial Y} \;+\; \underbrace{\frac{\partial F}{\partial Q} \cdot \frac{\partial Q^*}{\partial Y}}_{= \; 0 \text{ at optimum!}}$$
$$\nabla_Y F_{\text{aligned}}(Y) = \frac{2 (Y - X_0 Q^*)}{\|X_0\|_F^2}$$

Profound Numerical Benefit: You never differentiate the SVD! The gradient is identical in shape to the anchored gradient, simply replacing $X_0$ with its rotated version $X_0 Q^*$.

Scale Preservation Identity: $\langle \nabla D, Y \rangle = 0$

Because $D(Y)$ is homogeneous of degree 0, Euler's theorem yields $\langle \nabla D, Y \rangle = 0$ identically. The orthogonality term cannot alter scale $\|Y\|_F$; scale is pinned cleanly by the fidelity term alone.

5. Optimization Dynamics: SPG & Trajectory Comparison

Spectral Projected Gradient on the non-negative orthant with and without alignment

Algorithm & Results

The SPG Optimization Loop (Birgin, Martínez & Raydan 2000)

  1. Barzilai-Borwein Spectral Step: $t_k = \frac{\langle s_k, s_k \rangle}{\langle s_k, r_k \rangle}$ with $s_k = Y_k - Y_{k-1}, \, r_k = g_k - g_{k-1}$.
  2. Armijo Backtracking on Projected Direction: Find step length $t$ such that $Y_+ = \Pi_{\ge 0}(Y - t g_k) = \max(0, Y - t g_k)$ satisfies: $$E_w(Y_+) \le E_w(Y) - \sigma \frac{\|Y_+ - Y\|_F^2}{t}$$
  3. Stationarity Certificate: Stops when gradient-mapping norm $\|G_{\text{map}}\| = \frac{\|Y_+ - Y\|_F}{t} \le \mathrm{tol}$.
Flow with align=False
  • $X_0$ is anchored rigidly.
  • Descent path fights against coordinate rotation.
  • Clamps heavily on $\{Y \ge 0\}$, causing loss of rank and degraded reconstruction.
Flow with align=True
  • $X_0 Q_t^*$ rotates dynamically with $Y_t$.
  • Flow slides effortlessly into non-negative disjoint cones.
  • Retains full subspace expressiveness while discovering sparse, nameable factors.

Key Takeaways

• Theoretical Consistency: $D(Y)$ is $O(k)$-invariant; align=True makes $F(Y)$ $O(k)$-invariant too.
• Auditable & Interpretable: Turns dense PCA loadings into sparse, disjoint, non-negative factors without heuristic loss weights.

align=False: Fixed orientation creates high resistance align=True: Procrustes rotation follows path of least resistance
