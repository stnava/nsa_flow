🔬 Numerical Precision & Stability Insights

  • Boundary Coordinate Noise Filtering: Coordinates at lower bound $x = \text{lo}$ can experience cancellation noise of order $10^{-22}$ during subspace truncation, which would otherwise falsely collapse $a_{\max}$ to 1.0. Applying strict boundary filtering restored identical step extrapolation to Python.
  • Plateau Termination Certification: Aligned plateau condition check with Python reference requiring stalled() certification over the energy window, preventing premature early stopping when the gradient mapping is actively descending.
  • Adaptive BLAS / Stack Dispatch: Problems with $p \le 128$ execute entirely within stack buffers with zero heap allocation; problems with $p > 128$ automatically route Gram products through BLAS GEMM for optimal cache line utilisation.
